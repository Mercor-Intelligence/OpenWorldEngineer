DATA SOURCES:			Measurements at the wind tunnel of the CMT-Clean Mobility & Thermofluid institute
						Numerical simulations carried out at the CMT-Clean Mobility & Thermofluid institute

NOTES:					Three different softwares where evaluated (Star-CCM+, Fluent and OpenFOAM)

NUMERICAL DATA:
---------------
StarCCM -> Results obtained using Star-CCM+
Fluent	-> Results obtained using Ansys Fluent
OF		-> Results obtained using OpenFOAM

vbm		-> Results from steady RANS using Virtual Blade Model
mrf		-> Results from steady RANS using Moving Reference Frame
urans	-> Results from unsteady RANS using Sliding Mesh
des		-> Results from Detached Eddy Simulation using Sliding Mesh

EXPERIMENTAL DATA:
------------------
exp		-> Measurements of the original COTS propeller. Measurements were produced with the flow at different angles (measured with respect to the axis of rotation). 0 degrees mean pure axial flight.

ACOUSTICS:
----------
Inside the results folder.
Contains acoustic experimental measurements at 5 different rotational velocities (at hover).
The microphones are located at a 2 m. radius and equispaced 30 degrees. 
Mic1 is 60 degrees below the plane of rotation. Mic2 is 30 degrees below the plane of rotation. Mic3 is at the plane of rotation. Mic4 is 30 degrees above the plane of rotation. Mic5 is 60 degrees above the plane of rotation. Mic 6 is 90 degrees above the plane of rotation (upstreams, at the axis of rotation).
